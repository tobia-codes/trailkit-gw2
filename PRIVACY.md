# Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-11 · [Deutsche Fassung](PRIVACY.de.md)

The trailkit addons do not send any data to us. There is no tracking, no
analytics and no automatic error report. This page explains what stays on
your PC and what leaves it.

**Contact:** <tobiacodes@gmail.com>

## Data on your PC

- **Your notes and settings** are stored only in the Nexus addons folder
  under `trailkit/`. They never leave your PC.
- **Logs** are stored under `trailkit/logs/`. They record when an addon ran,
  its version, your Windows version and game language, what the addon did
  and which errors happened. They never contain the content of your notes
  and never folder paths. Each addon keeps the newest 14 days with a log and
  deletes anything older than 30 days.
- You can delete the `trailkit` folder at any time. That removes all of
  this data.

## Update check by Nexus

When the game starts or you load an addon, Raidcore Nexus checks
`gw2.trailkit.de` for a new version. That site is hosted on GitHub Pages by
GitHub, Inc. (USA). Like every web request, this transmits your IP address
and technical request data to GitHub. We do not receive these data and
cannot see who checked.

- **Purpose and legal basis:** delivering bug fixes and updates, based on our
  legitimate interest in keeping the addons working and safe
  (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Recipient:** GitHub, Inc., which stores the IP addresses of GitHub Pages
  visitors for security purposes under its own responsibility. We have no
  access to them and store nothing ourselves.
- **Transfer to the USA:** GitHub states that it relies on the EU-U.S. Data
  Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses. GitHub's privacy
  statement:
  <https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>.
- You are not obliged to allow this. Without it, Nexus cannot install
  updates. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

## Reporting a problem

- **Report a problem** opens a form on GitHub in your browser. Only the
  addon name and version are put into the link. Nothing is sent before you
  submit the form yourself.
- You need a GitHub account. **Issues are public:** everyone can read your
  report and anything you attach. GitHub's privacy statement applies.
- **Export log** only saves a text file in your Downloads folder. You decide
  whether you attach it. Please check it before you do.

## Planned: trailkit server

A server at `api.trailkit.de` (Hetzner, Germany) is planned. It will tell
the addons whether an update is required and may later accept problem
reports sent directly from the addon. It is not active yet. This page will
be completed before it starts.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of
processing and data portability (Art. 15–20 GDPR), and the right to lodge a
complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one
where you live. Because the addons store your data only on your PC, you can
view and delete it yourself. For data held by GitHub, contact GitHub. For
anything else, use the contact above.

> **Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR).** You can object at any time, for
> reasons arising from your particular situation, to the update check,
> which is based on our legitimate interest. The simplest way is to remove
> the addon; you can also write to the contact above.
