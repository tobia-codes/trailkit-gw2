## Searching

memo has two searches: the **search** above the note list finds notes by the words in their title or text; **find in the note** finds a text in the open note.

### Using the search

Show the **Search** field from the menu or press **Shift twice** quickly, on its own (not while editing); it stays above the note list until you close it, also after a restart. The **×** in the field first clears what you typed; on the empty field it closes the search. **Shift twice** again closes it at once. Type into it: the list shows only notes that contain every word you typed — in their title or text, upper or lower case alike. Umlauts count as their own letters: "ä" finds "Ä", but not "a".

- **×** in the field or **Escape** (while you type in the field) clears the search; a second Escape closes the window. Hiding the field also clears the search. The search stays when you close and reopen the window, until the game restarts.
- You cannot drag entries while searching; clear the search first. Renaming, the right-click menu and *Move to* work as usual. A new note or group clears the search.

### What the results show

- Groups with hits open by themselves; their number shows how many notes match. A group whose name matches shows everything inside.
- Hits are marked in the titles and in the open note, which jumps to the first hit. Notes that match only through their text show the number of hits at the end of the row.
- Link texts and chat-link codes are searched, link addresses are not. A chip whose code matches gets a frame.
- The list shows only matches. The open note stays open on the right even when it does not match; click a match to open it.

### Find in the note

Press **Ctrl+F** while a note is open — also while editing — or choose *Find in note* in the menu of the three dots next to *Edit*. A bar opens below the toolbar; type a text, upper or lower case alike. The current hit is filled in color, the others are framed, and the bar shows where you are, e.g. *2 of 5*.

- **Enter** or **F3** goes to the next hit, **Shift+Enter** or **Shift+F3** to the previous one; after the last hit it starts at the first again. The arrows in the bar do the same.
- While editing, the current hit is selected, so you can type over it.
- The bar offers the text you selected in the editor, else the search of the list, else your last find.
- **Ctrl+F** again, **Escape** or the **×** closes the bar; after that, Escape closes the window.
