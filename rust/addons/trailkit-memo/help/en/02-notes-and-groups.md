## Notes and groups

Your notes sit in a list that you can sort and organize in groups.

### Creating and renaming

- **New note / new group:** the two buttons above the list. The new entry goes right after the selected note, or into the selected group as its last entry; without a selection it goes to the end of the list. The list scrolls to it. A click on an empty spot of the list selects nothing; the note on the right stays. Type a name right away and press Enter.
- **Right-click** a group or an empty spot of the list for the same actions: on a group the new entry goes into it, on an empty spot at the end of the list.
- **Rename:** double-click an entry, or right-click › *Rename*. Enter or clicking elsewhere keeps the new name, Escape cancels.
- **Note menu:** the button with three dots next to *Edit* offers *Rename*, *Move to*, *Reset type* and *Delete* for the open note. *Rename* opens the editor with the title selected; finish with *Save*.

### Sorting and moving

- **Reorder:** drag a note or group to a new place. A line shows where it lands. At the top edge of a group it goes above the group; at the bottom edge it goes below a closed group or becomes the first entry of an open one. Below the last entry it goes to the end of the list. Drop on the middle of a group, which gets a frame, to put it at the group's end; rest there a moment and a closed group opens. A red "no entry" sign means it cannot go there (a group cannot go into itself). Near the top or bottom edge the list scrolls. Escape cancels.
- **Move to:** right-click › *Move to* › *Top level* or a group moves the entry to the end of it, without dragging.

### Groups

Groups have bold names and show how many notes they contain on the right; a thin line on the left runs from an open group down to its last entry. Click anywhere on a group's row to open or close it; the arrow does it at once, the rest of the row a moment later, so a double-click renames the group without closing it. The note you are reading stays on the right.

### Deleting

Right-click › *Delete*, or select the entry and press **Delete** while the list has the focus. The entry goes to the trash; a group with notes asks first. The note you are editing cannot be deleted until you save or cancel. How to get it back is under *Trash and undo*.
