## Keyboard shortcuts

These are the keys memo uses, sorted by where they work.

### Window

- **Shift twice** – show or close the search (not while editing)
- **Ctrl+F** – open or close find in the open note (also while editing)
- **Enter** / **F3** – next hit while finding; with **Shift** the previous one
- **Escape** – close an open menu or the find bar first, clear the search while you type in it; otherwise close the window (with unsaved changes memo asks first)
- **Ctrl+Z** – undo the last delete or move
- **Ctrl+Y** or **Ctrl+Shift+Z** – do it again
- **Ctrl+mouse wheel** – make all text larger or smaller
- **Ctrl+0** – default text size
- **Escape** in the help window – close the help window

### Reading a note

- **Drag** – select text; **Shift+click** extends the selection
- **Ctrl+A** – select the whole note
- **Ctrl+C** – copy the selected text

### Note list

- **Delete** – move the selected entry to the trash
- **Double-click** – rename an entry
- **Enter** – keep the new name
- **Escape** – cancel renaming or dragging

### Editor

- **Ctrl+B** – bold
- **Ctrl+K** – add or change a link
- **Ctrl+I** – italic
- **Ctrl+S** – save
- **Ctrl+Z** – undo your typing
- **Backspace** – next to a chat link chip: delete the whole chip
- **Backspace** – right after a list or checkbox marker: remove the whole marker, the text stays
- **Backspace** / **Delete** – on a bracket or parenthesis that belongs to a link: delete the whole link
