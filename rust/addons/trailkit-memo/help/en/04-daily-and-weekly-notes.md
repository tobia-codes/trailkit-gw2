## Daily and weekly notes

Daily and weekly notes untick their checkboxes at the game's reset.

### Choosing the kind

Choose the kind of a note in the box next to its title (*No reset*, *Daily reset*, *Weekly reset*), or right-click it in the list › *Reset type*. At the game's daily or weekly reset all checkboxes in such a note are unticked again — handy for dailies and weekly clears. A note you switch to daily or weekly keeps its ticks until the next reset.

### When notes reset

- **Calendar icons** at the right end of a row: a calendar with a **1** marks a daily note (reset at 00:00 UTC), a calendar with **dots** a weekly note (reset on Mondays at 07:30 UTC). Hover the icon to see how long it is until the next reset.
- If the game was closed at reset time, memo resets the note the next time you open the window — once, however many resets you missed.
- While you edit a note (or it has a draft), its reset waits until you save or discard; your text is never changed under your hands.
